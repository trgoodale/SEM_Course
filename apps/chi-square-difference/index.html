χ² difference test

In SEM, two models are nested when one can be made from the other by fixing or equating parameters, for example by constraining loadings to be equal across groups. The χ² difference test asks whether the constraints make fit significantly worse.

Result

Looking back to R3. This is the same logic as the F test for the change in R² in a hierarchical regression. There, Model 1 was Model 2 with some paths fixed at zero, and the test asked whether the extra paths explained more than chance would. Here the constrained model has more degrees of freedom (fewer free parameters), and the test asks whether freeing those parameters improves fit by more than chance would.
