Why this happens

With standardised items, each correlation equals the product of two loadings: r12 = λ1λ2, r13 = λ1λ3, r23 = λ2λ3. Solving these gives

λ1² = r12 × r13 / r23 λ2² = r12 × r23 / r13 λ3² = r13 × r23 / r12 error variance of item i = 1 − λi²

Nothing stops λ2 exceeding 1. It happens when an item correlates more strongly with the other two than they correlate with each other: the model can only explain that by giving the item more than all of its own variance.
