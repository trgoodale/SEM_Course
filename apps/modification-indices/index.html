Residuals and modification indices

A two-factor CFA with six indicators is fitted by maximum likelihood to simulated data. The population may contain a misspecification you cannot see. Use the residuals and modification indices to look for it, free parameters, and then test whether your changes hold up in a new sample.

Modification indices

Each value is the drop in χ² if that parameter were freed (found here by actually refitting; Amos reports a quick approximation of the same thing). The expected change is the estimate the parameter would take.

Do your changes replicate?

Fit your modified model to a fresh sample from the same population. Parameters freed only because of chance will usually not be needed in the new sample (MacCallum et al., 1992).

Questions for your SEM log

  1. With no misspecification in the population, free the largest modification index three times. How much does χ² improve, and do the freed parameters replicate in a new sample?
  2. Set the population to contain a cross-loading. Which residuals and which modification index point to it? Does freeing it replicate?
  3. Why should every freed parameter be justified by theory as well as by a modification index?
  4. An error covariance and a cross-loading can sometimes improve fit by similar amounts. How would you decide between them?
