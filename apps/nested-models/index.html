Nested regression models

A hierarchical regression compares two models. Model 1 contains the control variables C1 and C2. Model 2 contains the same controls plus two new predictors, X1 and X2. Model 1 is nested in Model 2: it is Model 2 with the paths from X1 and X2 fixed at zero. The F test for the change in R² asks whether the new predictors add anything.

Population

The effects are standardised. Each sample is drawn from this population and both models are fitted to it.

Arrow labels are the estimated unstandardised coefficients (b) in this sample. To keep the diagrams readable, the curved arrows for the correlations among the predictors are not drawn.

Questions for your SEM log

  1. Set the effects of X1 and X2 to zero and draw several new samples. How often is the F change significant? What should happen in the long run?
  2. Keep the effects of X1 and X2 small and increase N. Why does the same small ΔR² become significant?
  3. Increase the correlation among the predictors. What happens to ΔR², even though the effects of X1 and X2 have not changed? Link this to unique and shared variance from R2.
  4. Compare the coefficients of C1 and C2 in Model 1 and Model 2. Why do they change when X1 and X2 are added?
