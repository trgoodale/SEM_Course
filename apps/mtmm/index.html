Questions for your SEM log

  1. Raise the method loading. Which blocks of the matrix change, and which Campbell–Fiske criterion fails first?
  2. What pattern would you expect if the traits were not distinct (trait correlation near 1)?
  3. How does a CFA model of the MTMM matrix (Byrne, chapter 10) improve on reading the matrix by eye?
