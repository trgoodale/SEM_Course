Invariance ladder

Questions for your SEM log

  1. Choose the scenario where every intercept in Group B is higher. Why can this not be told apart from a latent mean difference unless the intercepts are constrained?
  2. With one non-invariant intercept, how large is the bias in the observed mean difference? What does partial scalar invariance let you do instead?
  3. Why must metric invariance hold before you compare correlations or regression paths across groups, and scalar invariance before you compare means?
