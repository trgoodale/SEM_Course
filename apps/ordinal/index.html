Ordinal items and thresholds

Likert-type items can be seen as cut-up versions of continuous latent responses. Set the true (latent) correlation between two items, choose the number of response categories and where the thresholds fall, and compare the Pearson correlation of the observed scores with the polychoric correlation.

Questions for your SEM log

  1. With symmetric thresholds, how many categories do you need before the Pearson correlation is within .05 of the latent correlation?
  2. What happens to the Pearson correlation when the two items are skewed in opposite directions? Why?
  3. Rhemtulla et al. (2012) suggest that items with five or more categories can often be treated as continuous. Does this app support that, and when might it not hold?
