Questions for your SEM log

  1. Under MCAR, which methods are unbiased? Which one is still wasteful, and why?
  2. Under MAR (missingness depends on X), why does FIML recover the mean of Y while listwise deletion does not?
  3. Under MNAR, does any method recover the truth? What could you do in a real study to make MAR more plausible?
  4. Why does mean imputation shrink the standard deviation of Y and the correlation, whatever the mechanism?

Part of the SEM with Amos study guide. FIML estimates here use the closed-form maximum-likelihood solution for this pattern (X complete, Y partly missing), which is what Amos's FIML gives for these data.
