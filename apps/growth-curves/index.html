Latent growth curve sandbox

In a latent growth model, an intercept factor and a slope factor describe each person's trajectory. Set their means, variances and covariance, choose the time codes (the fixed slope loadings), and see the trajectories and the means and variances the model implies at each wave.

Questions for your SEM log

  1. Change the time coding so that 0 is the last wave. What does the intercept now represent, and why do its mean and its covariance with the slope change?
  2. Free the loading for wave 3 (set it to something other than 2). What kind of change over time does this allow, and how would you interpret it?
  3. Why do the implied variances change over time when the slope has variance? What would they look like if the slope variance were 0?
