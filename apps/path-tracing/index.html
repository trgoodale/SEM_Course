The tracing rules

For a standardised recursive model, the correlation between two variables is the sum, over every allowable route between them, of the product of the path values along the route. A route is allowable if it:

  • does not go forward along an arrow and then backward against another,
  • passes through each variable at most once, and
  • includes at most one curved (two-headed) arrow.

Questions for your SEM log

  1. Set every path except a1 and b to zero. Why is the implied correlation between X1 and Y now exactly a1 × b?
  2. Turn on the X1 → Y path. How does the decomposition of r(X1, Y) change, and which part is the indirect effect?
  3. Why is part of r(M, Y) called spurious? What does that mean for interpreting a correlation between a mediator and an outcome?
  4. Each removed path adds one degree of freedom. Why?
